Cyber Fraud Advisory: Top 4 Scams & Prevention

1. Digital Arrest Fraud: Scammers pose as CBI/Customs officials on Skype/WhatsApp video calls claiming your Aadhaar is linked to drugs. Law enforcement NEVER arrests citizens over video calls! Hang up immediately.
2. Fake Electricity Bill / APK Links: SMS stating your power will be cut tonight unless you download an APK. These APKs install remote screen-share malware that empties bank accounts.
3. Part-Time Hotel Rating / YouTube Likes Scam: Initial payouts of Rs. 200, followed by demands for lakhs in crypto/VIP deposits.
The Golden Hour Rule: If you are defrauded, dial 1930 immediately. Reporting within 2 hours enables Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) to freeze fraudulent bank transfers before withdrawal.

Women, Child & Tourist Safety Advisory

Puducherry Pink Patrol Squads: Dedicated all-women police teams patrol Promenade Beach, bus stands, and college clusters. Dial 1091 for immediate mobile assistance.
Rock Beach Safety Caution: Due to heavy tidal undercurrents and submerged basalt boulders, swimming along Goubert Avenue is strictly prohibited and dangerous.
Authorized Rental Two-Wheelers: Rent only yellow-number-plate commercial bikes from government-registered agencies. Always wear BIS helmets (mandatory across Puducherry UT).
Child Protection: Any case of child harassment, unattended children near beaches, or child labor should be immediately reported to 1098.
